# Poke recipe


## Ingredients

- 1/2 avocado
- 200g salmon or ahi-tuna
- little soy sauce
- little sesame oil
- sesame seeds
1/2 red onion
1/2 green onion
20g grated ginger
1 lime

## Instructions

1. Rinse the vegetables.
2. Cut the fish and the vegetables.
3. Put them in a bowl. Sparkle with sesame seeds.
4. Add some sauce and oil.
5. Mix thoroughly
6. Repeat 4 and 5 until the texture is suitably oily.
